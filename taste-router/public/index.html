            How the agent works

            1 · Plan

            An LLM reads your brief and extracts the concept, the city and 3–5 cultural references that capture its vibe.

            2 · Ground

            References are resolved to Qloo entities. If too few exist, the agent reflects and swaps in better-known stand-ins.

            3 · Map taste

            Qloo's heatmap shows where that taste concentrates, street by street; taste DNA and demographics tell you who they are.

            4 · Scout

            For each hotspot it finds the real neighbourhood, taste-matched anchor venues, and how many of your category already exist.

            5 · Rank

            Opportunity = taste concentration vs. category saturation. "White space" beats "proven but crowded".

            6 · Write

            The LLM writes a launch brief using only Qloo evidence: programming from cross-domain picks, collabs, risks.
